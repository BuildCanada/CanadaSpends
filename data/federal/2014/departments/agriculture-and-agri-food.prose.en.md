Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada is the federal department responsible for the agriculture, agri-food and agri-based products sector. Its mandate includes supporting farm income stability, funding agricultural research and innovation, promoting the growth of domestic and export markets, and coordinating food safety and animal and plant health matters with other federal bodies. The department works with provinces, territories, and industry partners to develop and deliver programming aimed at helping the sector manage risk, adapt to changing conditions, and remain competitive. The department also oversees two related organizations: the Canadian Grain Commission, which regulates the handling of grain in Canada, establishes and maintains grain grading and quality standards, and protects the interests of grain producers, and the Canadian Dairy Commission, which works with provinces and industry to administer the national dairy supply management system and support stable returns for dairy producers.
