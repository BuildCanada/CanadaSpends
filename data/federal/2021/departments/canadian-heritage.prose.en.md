Its portfolio includes funding and oversight of national cultural institutions, support for artists and cultural organizations, promotion of Canada's two official languages, and programs related to sport and multiculturalism. Much of this work is delivered in partnership with arm's-length bodies such as public broadcasters, arts councils, and heritage institutions.
