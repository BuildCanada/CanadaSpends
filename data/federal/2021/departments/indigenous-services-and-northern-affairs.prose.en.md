Indigenous Services and Crown-Indigenous Relations covers two related federal departments responsible for the government's relationship with First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples. The Department of Indigenous Services delivers programs in areas such as health, education, child and family services, and community infrastructure on reserve and in Inuit and northern communities. The Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs is responsible for treaty relationships, land claims and self-government negotiations, and northern and Arctic affairs.
