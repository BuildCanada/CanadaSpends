Its responsibilities include administering the Employment Insurance program, delivering seniors' benefits, and supporting labour market programming, and it works alongside related bodies including the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, the Canadian Accessibility Standards Development Organization, and the Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety.
