The Democratic Institutions portfolio supports the administration of federal elections, the oversight of parliamentary security and intelligence review, and initiatives related to the integrity of Canada's electoral and democratic processes. It includes bodies responsible for organizing general elections and by-elections, reviewing national security matters on behalf of Parliament, and administering televised leaders' debates during federal election campaigns.
