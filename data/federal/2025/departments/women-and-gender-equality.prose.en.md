Women and Gender Equality Canada is the federal department responsible for advancing gender equality, including matters related to sex, sexual orientation, and gender identity or expression. It funds community organizations, supports research, and administers programs addressing gender-based violence and barriers to women's full participation in Canadian economic, social, and political life. Much of its work is carried out through funding provided to external organizations rather than through large internal operations.

Transfer payments make up the majority of the department's activity, delivered mainly through contribution and grant programs to external organizations. The largest of these was the Equality for Sex, Sexual Orientation, Gender Identity and Expression Program, which supports capacity building and community-level work for Two-Spirit, lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, intersex, and additional sexually and gender-diverse people in Canada. Other major programs included the Women's Program, which funds women's and other voluntary organizations working to further women's participation in Canadian society, and the Gender-based Violence Funding Program, which supports organizations and other levels of government in developing responses to gender-based violence.
