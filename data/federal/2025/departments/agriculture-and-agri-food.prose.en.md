The federal agriculture and agri-food portfolio supports the farming, food, and agri-based products sector in Canada. It develops policy and programs related to farm income stability, agricultural trade, food safety research, and sector innovation, and carries out regulatory functions for grain handling and dairy markets through affiliated agencies.

Among its largest transfer payments are contributions for the AgriInsurance and AgriStability programs under the Farm Income Protection Act, funding delivered jointly with provinces and territories under the Sustainable Canadian Agricultural Partnership, and grant and contribution payments supporting the dairy sector, including the Dairy Direct Payment Program and the AgriInvest program.
