Public Services and Procurement is the federal government's common service provider, supplying administrative and operational support to other departments and agencies. Its work includes managing federal real property and public works, purchasing goods and services on behalf of the government, administering public service pay and pension systems, and providing translation services. Because much of its activity supports the internal operations of other parts of government rather than delivering services directly to the public, its role is largely behind the scenes.
