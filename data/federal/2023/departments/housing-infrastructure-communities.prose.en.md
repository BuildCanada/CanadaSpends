Housing, Infrastructure and Communities is the federal portfolio responsible for supporting public infrastructure, housing supply and affordability, and community development across Canada. It funds transportation, water and wastewater, and other public infrastructure projects delivered by provinces, territories, municipalities, and Indigenous communities, and works to expand the supply of affordable housing. The portfolio includes delivery bodies such as the Office of Infrastructure of Canada, which administers many of the federal government's infrastructure funding programs, and the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, which supports housing finance and affordability initiatives.
