National Defence is the federal portfolio responsible for Canada's military, covering the Canadian Armed Forces and the civilian department that supports them. Its work spans defence policy, procurement of military equipment, and the day-to-day administration of Canada's armed services, alongside related bodies that provide intelligence, oversight, and grievance-resolution functions for the defence community.
