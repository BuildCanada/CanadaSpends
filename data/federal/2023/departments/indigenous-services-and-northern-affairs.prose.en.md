Indigenous Services and Crown-Indigenous Relations is the federal portfolio responsible for delivering services to First Nations, Inuit, and Métis communities and for managing the Crown's relationship with Indigenous peoples, including land claims, self-government negotiations, and northern affairs. It is organized around two departments: the Department of Indigenous Services, which delivers programs such as health care, education, and community infrastructure, and the Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs, which handles treaty and claims negotiations and northern governance.

The Department of Indigenous Services was the portfolio's largest spending entity, followed by the Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs, with smaller amounts attributed to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario and the Canadian High Arctic Research Station.
