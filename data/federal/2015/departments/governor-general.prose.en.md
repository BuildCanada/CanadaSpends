The Governor General is the federal office responsible for representing the Crown in Canada. The Governor General's duties are constitutional and ceremonial, including summoning, proroguing, and dissolving Parliament, granting royal assent to legislation passed by Parliament, presiding over the swearing-in of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, receiving foreign heads of state and diplomats on behalf of Canada, and recognizing achievement and service to the country through the Canadian honours system administered by the Chancellery of Honours at Rideau Hall. The office also maintains and operates Rideau Hall and the Citadelle in Quebec City, the Governor General's official residences.
