Agriculture and Agri-Food is the federal department responsible for Canada's agriculture, agri-food, and agri-based products sector. Its general mandate covers agricultural policy development, scientific research and innovation, trade and market development, environmental sustainability initiatives, and programs that help producers manage income stability and business risk. The department works alongside provinces, territories, industry associations, and producer groups to support the sector's competitiveness and its capacity to respond to changing market, trade, and weather conditions, and it oversees related federal organizations involved in grain quality assurance and dairy supply management regulation. Much of its programming is delivered jointly with provincial and territorial governments under shared agricultural policy frameworks that periodically renew the sector's suite of income stabilization, innovation, and market development programming.
