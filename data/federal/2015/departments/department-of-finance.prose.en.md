The Department of Finance is the federal department responsible for fiscal policy, the federal budget, and the overall management of the government's finances. Its mandate includes economic and financial analysis, oversight of the financial sector, and administration of major statutory transfer payments to provinces and territories, as well as support for a number of related financial sector agencies and regulatory bodies. Because several of the government's largest transfer programs to provinces and territories are administered through Finance, its reported spending tends to be dominated by those statutory obligations rather than by discretionary departmental programming.
