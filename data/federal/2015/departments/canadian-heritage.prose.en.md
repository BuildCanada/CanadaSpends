Canadian Heritage is the federal department responsible for supporting Canada's culture, arts, heritage, official languages, sport, and multiculturalism. Its mandate also extends to broadcasting and communications policy, and it oversees or funds a range of national museums, cultural agencies, and regulatory bodies operating in these areas, several of which report their own spending separately from the department. This portfolio structure means the department's own budget represents only part of the total federal effort in these cultural and heritage fields.
