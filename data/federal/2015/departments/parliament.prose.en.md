Parliament is not a conventional government department but the legislative branch of the Government of Canada, made up of the elected House of Commons and the appointed Senate, together with the officials and institutions that support their work. Members of the House of Commons debate and vote on legislation and hold the government to account, while senators provide a complementary chamber of review for bills passed by the Commons. It comprises the House of Commons and the Senate, along with the Library of Parliament, which provides non-partisan research, analysis, and information services to both chambers, and a number of smaller offices that support the integrity and functioning of Parliament, such as the Office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner, which oversees ethical conduct rules for members of the House of Commons, and the Senate Ethics Officer, who performs a similar function for senators.
