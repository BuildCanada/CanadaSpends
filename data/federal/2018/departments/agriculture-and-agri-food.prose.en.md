Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada supports Canada's agriculture and agri-food sector, working with provinces, territories, and industry to help producers manage income and production risks, invest in innovation and competitiveness, and access domestic and export markets. The department administers programs delivered jointly with provincial and territorial governments under federal-provincial agricultural policy frameworks.

The department's largest transfer payments supported the Business Risk Management suite of programs delivered under the federal-provincial Growing Forward 2 framework. Other significant contributions funded cost-shared strategic initiatives under Growing Forward 2 in both agricultural innovation and competitiveness and market development, along with contribution payments to the AgriInnovation program, which supports the development and commercialization of new agri-food products, technologies, and processes.
