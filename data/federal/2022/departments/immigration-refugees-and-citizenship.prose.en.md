Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) is the federal department responsible for processing applications for immigration, temporary residence, refugee protection, and Canadian citizenship. It sets and manages annual immigration levels, works with provinces and territories on settlement and integration programming for newcomers, and delivers passport services. The department also administers Canada's refugee protection and resettlement programs, covering both claims made within Canada and refugees resettled from abroad.
